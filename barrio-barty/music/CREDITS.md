# Built-in music: sources and licences

The two built-in tracks, one per music slot. Both are by Komiku and released
under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); each track page on Free
Music Archive says "licensed under a CC0 1.0 Universal License". No credit is
required; the Music settings name the artist anyway. Each file was re-encoded
from FMA's 320 kbps mp3 to 128 kbps stereo mp3 and loudness-normalised
(ffmpeg `loudnorm`, -20 LUFS), metadata stripped. Downloaded 2026-11-10.

Instrumental: Komiku's catalogue is instrumental chiptune and folk game
soundtracks; the Poupi album is tagged Instrumental on FMA. FMA's per-track
"Instrumental" flag says No on both, and that flag is known to be unreliable,
so listen to both before a game night.
