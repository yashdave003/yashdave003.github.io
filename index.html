Yash Dave

I'm a master's student in Computational and Mathematical Engineering at Stanford and a researcher at the Stanford Trustworthy AI Research (STAIR) Lab. My research is on AI measurement science: I study how AI systems are evaluated, how those evaluations sit within a broader ecosystem of benchmarks and incentives, and what they actually tell us about behavior in context. More broadly, I work to bridge technical and policy questions in AI, and I'm most interested in problems that need both.

Before Stanford, I studied Applied Math and Computer Science with a Public Policy minor at UC Berkeley. I worked with Prof. Alexander Strang on validating a hierarchical Bayesian model for inverse imaging problems, and spent several semesters teaching data science to Berkeley undergrads.
